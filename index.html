Write a prompt for an AI prototyping platform

Fill in the steps to build a prompt for a clickable prototype you can put in front of real users. It's tuned for Google Stitch and Lovable. It also works with similar online platforms that give you a visual canvas or live preview, such as Bolt.new, v0 or Figma Make. Start from your research, user stories and wireframes. The AI builds the prototype, but only your testing can tell you whether the design works.
